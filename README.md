# Kalenteri

A printable Finnish A4 wall calendar with a quote and generated artwork for every month.

- Week numbers, Finnish name days, public holidays and flag days, worked out for **any year**
- Pick the year, the first month and how many months (1–24)
- Every month: change the background pattern, roll a new one, upload your own picture, or pick a font
- Click any quote, author, month name, holiday or name day to rewrite it, and add your own notes
- Everything is saved in your own browser; print to A4 with no margins

`Kalenteri_2027.pdf` is the finished 2027 edition.

Live: https://labs.amarald.io/kalenteri

Name days: Almanakkatoimisto, University of Helsinki.
